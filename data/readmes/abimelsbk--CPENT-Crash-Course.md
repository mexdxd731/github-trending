## 📖 About This Repository

Preparing for the **C|PENT AI (Certified Penetration Testing Professional)** certification can be overwhelming due to the vast amount of topics, labs, documentation, and learning resources.

This repository brings everything together into a **single structured roadmap**, helping you prepare through:

- 📅 A complete 30-day study plan
- 🎥 Curated YouTube learning resources
- 📚 Official documentation and articles
- 🧪 Hands-on labs and vulnerable machines
- 📝 Practical learning path aligned with the C|PENT AI syllabus

Whether you're preparing for the certification or simply improving your penetration testing skills, this repository provides a clear roadmap from beginner concepts to advanced offensive security.
