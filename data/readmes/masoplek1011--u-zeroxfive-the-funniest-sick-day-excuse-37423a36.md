One line, under 200 characters, that someone could text their boss to call in
sick — a slept-wrong neck, and a twist right at the end.

Run it with Node 22:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```

It writes the single excuse line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`,
deterministically, every time.
