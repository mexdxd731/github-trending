## 🛠️ How It Works

GameFPS Booster applies a combination of system‑level tweaks that are both safe and effective:

- **CPU**: sets high priority, disables core parking, adjusts affinity for the game process.
- **GPU**: forces maximum performance power plan, optimizes shader cache, and disables unnecessary visual effects.
- **Memory**: clears standby list, reduces RAM usage, and adjusts pagefile settings.
- **Registry**: tunes TCP/IP parameters, disables Nagle's algorithm, and optimizes system responsiveness.
- **Processes**: kills background bloatware, disables Windows telemetry and notifications during gameplay.
- **Graphics**: forces exclusive fullscreen, disables V‑Sync, and enables GPU hardware scheduling.

All changes are **fully reversible** – a single click restores all system defaults.
