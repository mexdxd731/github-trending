## 🧭 What's inside

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎯 Aim suite
Per-weapon presets, bind keys, lock-on, bone targeting and entity-category filters.

**Field of view & lead** — range cap, adaptive FOV, smoothing curve, ballistic lead, switch cooldown.

### 👁 Entity overlays
**Characters & NPCs** — bounding boxes, skeletons, nicknames, crews, roles, HP, armor, held weapon, range.

**Vehicles & cameras** — ride, owner, driver, speed readout; static cameras with their coverage cones.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 💰 Loot overlay
Granular filters across guns, rounds, plating, gear, freight and spawn points.

### 📡 Radar
Zoom, range rings, FOV wedge, opacity slider and markers for off-screen contacts.

### ⚙️ Handling
**Recoil / sway / spread** — independent tuning for each.

**Combat mode** — single hotkey, condensed HUD, hide any element you want.

### 🧩 Profiles & UI
Saved configs, themes, locales, DPI scaling, VSync toggle and an FPS cap.

</td>
