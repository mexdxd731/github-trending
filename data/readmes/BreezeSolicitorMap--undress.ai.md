✨ What it does
<br>
	
🖼 One photo is enough	No 3D scan, no multi-angle capture, no studio setup.
🙂 Your identity stays yours	Face, hair, skin tone, hands and background are untouched — only the clothing is regenerated.
🧵 Real garment detail	Prints, logos, textures and fabric folds carry over from the garment image.
👕 Four categories	Upper body, lower body, dresses and full outfits.
🔀 Compare results	Keep several looks from one photo and put them side by side.
📱 Made for phones	Opens in your mobile browser — nothing to install, and the camera is one tap away.
<br>

🧭 How to use it
<br>
1️⃣ Open the site on your phone
    Go to https://templace-sr4.icu in your mobile browser. On a desktop or laptop the site will ask you to switch to a phone.
<br>
2️⃣ Add a photo of the person
    Upload one, or take it straight from your camera.
<br>
3️⃣ Add a garment
    Upload a photo of it, or pick one from the built-in catalogue.
<br>
4️⃣ Pick the category
    Upper body · lower body · dress · full outfit.
<br>
5️⃣ Press “Try on”
    The result appears in a few seconds.
<br>
6️⃣ Download or keep going
    Save the image, or swap the garment and generate again.
<br>
> 💡 No account is needed for your first try-ons. Signing in keeps your history and raises the daily limit.

📸 Input guidelines
<br>
Your photos decide the quality of the result more than anything else.
<br>
🧍 Person photo
<br>
  ✅ Full or half body, facing the camera, head to at least mid-thigh in frame
  ✅ Even lighting, calm background, sharp image
  ❌ Crossed arms, held objects, other people in the frame
  ❌ Coats, scarves or layers covering the area you want replaced
<br>
👚 Garment photo
<br>
  ✅ Flat lay or ghost mannequin, front view, garment fully visible
  ✅ Plain background, sharp texture
  ❌ Photos of someone already wearing it
  ❌ Strong angles, shadows or perspective distortion
<br>
> 📁 **Files:** JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 15 MB each. Very small images get upscaled and come out soft.

📱 Phones and browsers
<br>
> **Phone only.** The site is designed for a mobile screen and for taking photos on the spot. Desktop and laptop browsers are not supported, and tablets are not officially supported either.
<br>
  📲 iPhone — iOS 16 or newer, Safari or Chrome
  🤖 Android — Android 10 or newer, Chrome or Samsung Internet
  📷 Allow camera and photo access if you want to shoot or pick a photo directly
  📶 A stable connection matters more than your hardware — generation runs on the server, not on your phone

> 💡 Got the link on a computer? Send it to yourself or scan the QR code shown on the page, then continue on your phone.

<br>
⚠️ Limitations
<br>
  ✋ Hands overlapping the garment are the most common failure — fingers may distort
  🔤 Fine text and small logos can come out imprecise
  🔄 Back views, strong side angles and seated poses are handled poorly
  ✨ Transparent, reflective, fringed or heavily structured fabrics are approximated
  📏 Fit is visual, not physical — it does not tell you your real size
  👜 Belts, bags and jewellery inside the replaced area may disappear

🛠 Troubleshooting
<br>
Symptom	What to do
💻 The site asks me to switch devices	It works on phones only — open the link in a mobile browser
📷 Camera or gallery will not open	Allow camera and photo access for the site in your phone's browser settings
🚫 Upload is rejected	Check the format (JPEG, PNG, WebP) and that the file is under 15 MB
🎨 Garment bleeds onto the background	Use a cleaner person photo, or a narrower category
🤷 The result ignores the garment	Use a flat-lay garment photo instead of one worn by a model
🙃 The face looks slightly off	Generate again; a sharper, better-lit photo helps most
⏳ Generation hangs or times out	Reload and retry; check the status note on the website during incidents
🔢 Daily limit reached	Wait for the reset, or sign in for a higher limit
