> [!IMPORTANT]
> How to Fish is 1–4 player co-op, PvE only. Options marked **`crew`** change the shared island for everyone — creature AI, boss health, weather, physics.
>
> **Host-only mode ships enabled** and blocks them while you are a guest. The game is at its best when four idiots are failing together, so run this with people who know.

## What it does

You crashed a boat into an island because you were drunk. Now you fish your way home — starting with clams and a borrowed rod, ending with a shotgun, a boss the size of the island, and a gambling problem.

Most of this menu targets the early hours, which are deliberately punishing: no gear, no money, a bird that takes your third crab in a row. **Birds never steal your catch** is not a joke option, it is the single most requested thing in the reviews. **Never lose a fish** removes the red-bar failure state without removing the reel-in fight itself.

The fishing minigame is the game, so the option that skips it is tagged **`bypass`** and ships off.

## Hotkeys

`Insert` opens the menu · `End` resets everything · `F1`–`F12` as above, all rebindable · arrow keys and `Enter` navigate without a mouse

> [!TIP]
> **The setup that keeps the game funny:** birds never steal your catch, never lose a fish, barrier break speed at 5x, sell price multiplier at 3x. Nothing tagged `crew`, nothing tagged `bypass`. You still fight every fish and shoot every boss — you just stop losing your third crab in a row and stop mashing M1 until your finger hurts.
>
> **Physics throw force at 20x** is the one to show your friends. It affects nothing important and it is extremely stupid.

> [!WARNING]
> **Rig the fish casino** touches in-game currency in a joke casino — there is no real money anywhere in this game. It is here for completeness, and it removes a gag rather than a challenge.
>
> Options tagged `save` write persistent progression. Weapons, islands and quest state all live in the save, so let the rolling backup run before you touch them.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Will I get banned?</summary>
No. How to Fish is PvE co-op with no PvP, no leaderboards and no anti-cheat. The only risk is being the person who trivialised the boss fight your friends were enjoying.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I stop the birds stealing my fish?</summary>
Yes, and it is bound to <code>F5</code> because it is the first thing everybody asks for. Client-side, so it changes nothing for anyone else.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it skip the fishing minigame?</summary>
Only if you enable instant catch or auto-perfect reel, both tagged <code>bypass</code> and both off by default. Barrier break speed is the middle ground — you still play the minigame, there is just less mashing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it work solo?</summary>
Yes. The game supports single-player, and with nobody else in the session every option is effectively client-side.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will it ruin the run for my friends?</summary>
It can, if you host with frozen creature AI or boss health at 5%. Host-only mode blocks all of that while you are a guest, and it is on by default.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Options stopped working after an update.</summary>
The game launched this week and is patching quickly. Patches move memory offsets — check the Releases page for a build matching your game version, and use read-only mode to see what still reads correctly.
</details>
